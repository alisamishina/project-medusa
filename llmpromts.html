Challenges

Creating SPARQL queries One of the main difficulties was creating effective SPARQL queries to find the chosen topic in the ARCO ontology. Obtaining precise results often required multiple attempts and a deeper understanding of the structure of the datasets and different ways of writing queries. Identifying gaps Another challenge was identifying missing connections and incomplete information within the resources. At first it difficult to decide what infoThis process required careful analysis of classes, properties, and relationships in order to better understand the existing data and detect gaps. The use of Large Language Models The use of Large Language Models (LLMs) was helpful during the research. In our project we used different LLMs for testing prompts and creating new RDF triples. Some LLMs provided incorrect triples while the other wrote the correct ones suitable specifically for ArCo database.
